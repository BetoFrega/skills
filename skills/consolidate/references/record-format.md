# Living record format

Read before consolidating catalogue records, shared rules, direction, proposals, or
current delivery observations. Adapt headings, language, fields, and objects to the
destination while retaining these invariants.

## Consolidate the current contract

State the latest approved contract directly in present tense. Separate proposals
and accepted future-effective rules, with timing; keep authority and sources in
compact metadata/evidence. Rules must be understandable without reconstructing interviews.

After acceptance, replace superseded rules and reconcile related sections, examples,
summaries, open questions, and records. Several saves for one choice yield one
consolidated result.

Revision history belongs in native versions, commits, or audit history. Prohibit edit
narratives anywhere in the living record: sections, footnotes, appendices, or comment
logs. Native history retains earlier values, question numbers, rejected answers, and
reopened choices. Current rationale may explain purpose/value;
[decision records](decisions.md) explain substantive choices, not edits. Dated,
scoped observations of current discrepancies remain evidence.

## Optional final changelog

Only substantial changes to outcomes, material rules, permissions, commercial terms,
scope, or availability qualify. Omit creation entries, formatting, wording, links,
routine observations, minor clarifications, and the section when unnecessary.

- Place last, after every other section.
- At most five recent entries, newest first; older entries stay in native history.
- One plain-text bullet/logical line per substantial change or coordinated set:
  `YYYY-MM-DD — <what changed>`.
- At most 20 whitespace-separated tokens after the bullet marker, including date.
  Count before saving and shorten violations.
- State only the change: no rationale, approval detail, question references, or
  earlier-value comparisons. Repeated saves combine; readbacks add nothing.

## Template and example

Adapt the [functionality template](../assets/functionality-template.md); read the
[worked example](functionality-example.md) when establishing representation or removing
interview accumulation. Its fictional rules/evidence are not product authority.

Prefer equivalent existing native fields. Keep user value/cost, business value/cost,
and implementation, deployment, authorized release, observed exposure, and flags
independently accessible. Explain relevant unknowns; omit irrelevant scaffolding.
Split materially different actor outcomes into identifiable records/sections under
product granularity.

## Completion checks

Read the whole saved document: rules/examples match accepted choices and timing;
titles/summaries/open questions agree; no edit/interview narration remains; actors,
jobs, behavior, four value/cost meanings, delivery dimensions, and relevant knowledge
gaps are independently accessible. Any changelog must qualify, be final, contain no
more than five entries, and respect each entry's word limit. Persistence alone is
insufficient.
