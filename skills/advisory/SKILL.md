# Advisory

Consult one independent advisor when another perspective can reduce uncertainty or
reveal risk, especially in the cases the description lists. Whether to consult is
your call, and it needs no per-call approval. A change that spans many layers is not
by itself a reason.

Choose the model with [model-selection](../model-selection/SKILL.md). Prefer Fable 5.1
for consequential decisions, diagnoses, plans, and reviews, and Opus 5.5 for stalls,
repeated errors, complex algorithms, or cross-layer interactions without such a
decision. These preferences override lowest-cost selection when the model is available
and compatible with the tools, context, and data retention; otherwise use the next
most capable model. User choices win; disclose what a fallback loses. Choose effort
separately and set both explicitly where supported. The Codex and Claude CLIs are
optional; check which ones are available and compatible.

Give the advisor the open question, evidence, constraints, and decision criteria.
When independence matters, leave out your own conclusion. The advisor investigates
and recommends; you decide and execute. Check the advice against the evidence and
raise material disagreement or risks that change the decision. Consulting does not
expand scope or permit changes, external actions, or further delegation.
