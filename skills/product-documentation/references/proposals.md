# Proposals and pitches

Read this to shape a product proposal or prepare an investment pitch. Use the format
and decision workflow configured for the product; a pitch is useful when an investment
choice warrants it, rather than as a required artifact for every change.

## Shape the investment

Ground the problem in affected actors, jobs, current behavior, and available evidence.
Separate the desired outcome from candidate solutions. Describe applicable content
in the configured native object:

- problem, audience, job, current contract, and why the outcome matters;
- desired behavior and the capability or functionality affected;
- appetite or investment constraint, such as time, capacity, or an agreed bound;
- proposed approach with enough shape to assess feasibility and unresolved risks;
- user value and user cost, business value and business cost, kept separate;
- material assumptions, dependencies, rabbit holes, and verification questions;
- exclusions or boundaries that keep the investment within its appetite;
- acceptance authority and the decision still required.

Treat appetite as an investment choice, not as an observed delivery estimate. Describe
behavior at a useful level while retaining room for implementation decisions.
Interaction sketches may communicate intent when explicitly illustrative and
replaceable. State any binding rule as behavior in the proposal, independent of a
particular control, screen, or click sequence.

## Connect to the product model

Raw ideas and untested hypotheses stay linked to their [discovery evidence](context.md).
A sufficiently defined proposal may enter the [catalogue](catalogue.md) with its own
proposal state. Preserve the current approved contract while evaluating an alternative.
Link the proposal to affected capabilities, rules, evidence, and earlier decisions.

When investment is accepted, use [decisions](decisions.md) to preserve the choice,
authority, and effective timing. Update affected product contracts only to the extent
actually accepted. An approved experiment or work budget may leave permanent product
behavior undecided. Create specs, scopes, and tickets when their work is requested.

Complete when the saved proposal expresses the investment and boundaries clearly,
material questions and assumptions are visible, and its relationships and decision
state are verified. Report remaining choices without presenting proposed behavior as
already implemented or released.
