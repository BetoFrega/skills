# Price the Experiment

An experiment is worth running only when its answer could change the decision and it
costs less than deciding, implementing, and adjusting later.

Before proposing an exploratory experiment, compare it with making a reversible
business decision and proceeding with an adjustment path. Weigh time, tokens,
attention, delay, execution, and expected rework; qualitative judgment is enough.

- Uncertainty alone is not a reason to experiment.
- When the experiment loses, obtain the needed business choice, proceed within
  authorization, and preserve changeability.
- Required correctness, security, and data-integrity verification stays proportional
  to the implementation, whatever the experiment decision.
